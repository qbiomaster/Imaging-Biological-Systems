# Oral presentation and BYS MCQ guidelines

For Build your setup in 2026-2027, assessment is individual and includes interactions during practicals, answers to instructors, and questions asked of other groups. A 45-minute multiple-choice questionnaire (MCQ) will take place after the BYS oral presentations.

## Advanced practicals

Presentations should be made with open-source software (Libre Office, google Slide, Slides, markdown, etc…).

Presentations should last 12 minutes (15 minutes for groups of three) and will be followed by 20 minutes of questions from the audience (students and instructors).

Students in groups should have an equivalent amount of presentation time.

The presentation should provide a synthetic but comprehensible view of their project to the other students of the Master.

### Assessment

- scientific quality of the oral presentation

- quality of the slides

- mastery of scientific vocabulary

- duration of their presentation.

- quality of their questions

- quality of their answers

## BYS practicals - Oral presentations

Presentations should be prepared using open-source or freely accessible software (LibreOffice, Google Slides, Markdown, etc.).

Presentations should last **10 minutes (15 minutes for groups of three)** and will be followed by **20 minutes of questions and discussions**.

Each group member should have an equivalent amount of presentation time.

### Objectives

The presentation should provide a **clear, concise and scientifically rigorous overview** of your project, accessible to the other students of the Master.

Beyond explaining how your setup works, you should communicate the reasoning and experimental process behind its design, construction and characterization, including the challenges encountered, the results obtained and the lessons learned.

You are expected to critically assess your work, discussing the reliability of your measurements, the performance and limitations of your setup, and possible improvements.

**The goal is not to present everything you did, but to select and explain what is most relevant to understanding your project.**

### Discussions and feedback

After each presentation, **all students in the audience are expected to participate actively** by:

- Providing constructive feedback on the presentation itself, highlighting both strengths and areas for improvement.
- Asking questions to clarify points they did not understand, deepen the discussion or explore the scientific and technical choices made by the presenting group.

Feedback should be respectful, specific and constructive. Asking questions is encouraged, including when something was unclear: the purpose is to help everyone learn.

### Assessment

Assessment is individual, even for group presentations, and will consider:

- Scientific understanding and critical thinking.
- Quality, clarity and structure of the presentation.
- Mastery of scientific vocabulary and respect for the allocated time.
- Relevance and quality of answers to questions.
- Active participation in discussions, including constructive feedback and questions addressed to other groups.

## BYS MCQ

A **45-minute multiple-choice questionnaire (MCQ)** will take place after the BYS oral presentations.

It covers the imaging techniques and principles introduced in the introductory lectures, optics practicals and Build Your Setup sessions. **The Advanced Microscopy part of the course is excluded.**

There will be **30 questions, with one correct answer per question**. No calculator is allowed. The questions will assess your understanding and scientific reasoning rather than your ability to memorise formulas.
