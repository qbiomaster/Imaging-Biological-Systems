# Oral presentation guidelines

For Build your setup in 2026-2027, assessment is individual and includes interactions during practicals, answers to instructors, and questions asked of other groups. A final multiple-choice quiz is under consideration.

## Advanced practicals

Presentations should be made with open-source software (Libre Office, google Slide, Slides, markdown, etc…).

Presentations should last 12 minutes (15 minutes for groups of three) and will be followed by 20 minutes of questions from the audience (students and instructors).

Students in groups should have an equivalent amount of presentation time.

The presentation should provide a synthetic but comprehensible view of their project to the other students of the Master.

### Assessment

- scientific quality of the oral presentation

- quality of the slides

- mastery of scientific vocabulary

- duration of their presentation.

- quality of their questions

- quality of their answers
