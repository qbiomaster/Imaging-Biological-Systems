# Advanced practicals — written report guidelines

These guidelines apply to the advanced microscopy report. No written report is required for Build your setup in 2026-2027.

The report should be written in font size 11, Times New Roman or Arial, with single line spacing, and reasonable margins (Top, Bottom 2.5 cm; Left, Right 1.9 cm). Text must be justified.

The layout can be in one or two columns.

Pages must be numbered.

## Report structure

### Cover page

- Name and Surname

- Project title

- Name and Surname of the supervisor

### Report

- Abstract: summary of the main points of the report, including the research question, methods, results, and conclusions

- General introduction: The subject must be placed in a general scientific context, the important concepts must be introduced and the problematic and objectives of the study must be clearly stated.

- Material and methods: describe the relevant methods such as the setups (with relevant equipment specifications), sample preparation, analysis, etc…

- Results: factual description of the rationale, results, and analysis of the experiments.

- Discussion: interpretation of the results in the context of the research question, including any limitations of the study and consistency of the results with the literature and theory.

- Conclusion: A summary of the main points and implications of the research.

- Bibliography: list of all sources cited in the article.

### Equations, tables, and figures

Equations, tables, and figures should all be numbered and referenced in the main text.

Equations shall be separated from the text and the terms must be explained.

Figures axes, labels, and units must be clearly visible. The figure should be accompanied by a legend containing a brief title that describes the main content of the figure and a concise but comprehensive description of what is shown in the figure. Any error bars or other statistical indicators must be explained.

### Annex (optional)

Annexes should not contain essential information and the report should be understandable without the annexes.

## Assessment

- your personal implication and your ability to understand and synthesize the context of your project.

- the general presentation of the project: writing of the introduction, the working hypothesis, the objectives of the work, experimental procedures, the methods and identification of strong and weak points, and the quality of the figures.

- a good level of understanding of your project and of the different methodological techniques that you developed and excellent mastery of scientific vocabulary.

## Additional tips

- Start writing the report as early as possible!

- Do not hesitate to contact your instructors if you have any questions.

- You should be able to justify why you chose this method and not another.

- Use online tools to check your spelling and grammar

- Do not use text, figures, schemes, etc… from others without citing them.
